Nuru Shimoni

Nuru shimoni is an interactive installation designed for the 2019 Rhizom festival in Zürich. It is meant to represent a portal like growth connecting its participants to a seemingly extensive Mycelial network of roots. Sonar sensors and Microphones were used to detect presence and communication. A smoke machine, bass shaker, sound system and 1920 mapped leds to immerse people in a responsive environment.
